# Protonup-qt
To download other proton versions (like cachyos or GE) use the protonup-qt app  
Either get it through flathub or through the package manager of your distro

Within the app you can download different kinds of proton  
In my experience the cachyos proton provides the best performance  
After you downloaded your desired version of proton restart steam and select it in the compatibility tab in properties
